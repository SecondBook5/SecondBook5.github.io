Research program

Inferring biological state from incomplete measurements

Human biology is usually observed indirectly, asynchronously, and after the fact. My work asks how far we can go from those measurements without hiding the assumptions required to get there.

Research programs

Different modalities, different failure modes

Each program starts from a different measurement system, but the standard is the same: the computational representation should earn its biological interpretation.

Method development · MSK

HostBio / Comp-MRF

Can routine CT become a quantitative measurement of host tissue state?

I developed a scan-adaptive probabilistic framework for tissue composition that explicitly handles partial-volume mixing, spatial dependence, nuisance components, and within-scan references. The resulting phenotypes are tested against immune, genomic, treatment, and outcome data rather than judged only by image appearance.

Master's thesis · Johns Hopkins

StageBridge

What can cross-sectional human tissue legitimately tell us about progression?

StageBridge represents regulatory state using transcription-factor and pathway activity, gene regulatory structure, and tissue context. A failed recoverability benchmark forced a redesign of the coupling and a narrower interpretation, making model criticism part of the method rather than a post hoc caveat.

Collaborative systems biology · MSK

Secondary lymphedema

Which tissue programs persist after chronic inflammatory injury, and which apparent signals are artifacts?

I analyze human single-cell and bulk transcriptomics, proteomics, and microbiome data in collaboration with the Mehrara laboratory. The project is both biological and methodological: persistent tissue-state claims must survive donor structure, composition, preprocessing, and cross-cohort measurement differences.
