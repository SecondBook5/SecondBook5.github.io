Scientific problem

What keeps tissue altered after the initiating injury?

Secondary lymphedema is not simply an acute inflammatory episode. Epithelial, stromal, immune, and lymphatic compartments remain remodeled, creating a useful system for studying persistent tissue state across multiple biological scales.

My role in the Mehrara laboratory is computational. I lead and contribute analyses that connect human single-cell transcriptomics, bulk RNA-seq, proteomics, microbiome data, and orthogonal experimental measurements to determine which tissue programs persist and which apparent differences disappear under more careful analysis.

Cell state

Separate redistribution of cell states from regulation within a state.

In paired human skin single-cell data, I use state-aware analyses to distinguish changes in cell abundance from transcriptional changes within established populations. This localized F2RL1/PAR2 enrichment to specific keratinocyte states rather than treating epidermal expression as a single bulk signal.

In a separate 79,054-cell atlas, the same logic identified an inflammatory, IL13-enriched mast-cell program consistent with remodeling within established mast-cell states. The distinction matters because a disease-associated signal can arise either from more of a cell state or from a true change within that state.

Multimodal validation

Use each modality for the question it can actually answer.

Bulk transcriptomics captures coordinated tissue programs, proteomics asks whether those programs are reflected at the protein level, and single-cell data localize them to specific cell states. In the capecitabine project, I integrated these molecular measurements with endothelial assays, genetic perturbation, lymphatic physiology, and spatial histopathology.

The combined analysis showed that valve and contractile remodeling accompanied impaired collecting-vessel structure and pumping rather than functional recovery. Related work links lymphedema-fluid signatures of epithelial-barrier loss, TGF-β/EMT, and tissue stress to spatially localized dermal-backflow pathology.

Measurement lesson

A large biological-looking difference can disappear when the measurement process is harmonized.

Across more than 500 microbiome samples from three cohorts, related datasets initially appeared to differ by nearly two-fold in taxonomic richness, approximately 210 versus 104 detected genera. After harmonizing classification, the counts were approximately 213 versus 203.

That discrepancy motivated Dysbioscope, a config-driven workflow for measurement-aware cross-cohort microbiome analysis. The lesson is broader than microbiome work: before explaining a striking difference biologically, test whether the pipeline created it.

Collaboration

Computational inference should remain anchored to the experimental system.

This work is deliberately collaborative. My contribution is the computational design, analysis, harmonization, and interpretation that connect molecular measurements to experiments performed by the Mehrara and Kataru teams.

Published work has examined PAR2-associated epidermal remodeling and the broader omic architecture of secondary lymphedema. Ongoing analyses and manuscripts are described here only at a level already represented in my public research record.
